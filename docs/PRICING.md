## ROI Calculation

**Without VERTEX**:
- 100 bookings/month
- 35% no-shows = 35 lost
- Avg price: $150 MXN
- Monthly loss: 35 × 150 = $5,250 MXN

**With VERTEX**:
- 100 bookings/month
- 8% no-shows = 8 lost
- Monthly loss: 8 × 150 = $1,200 MXN
