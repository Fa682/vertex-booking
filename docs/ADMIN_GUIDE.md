# ADMIN GUIDE - VERTEX BOOKING

## Overview
The Admin Dashboard allows business owners to manage their daily appointments, track metrics, and reduce no-shows.

## Managing Appointments
1. **View Today's Bookings**: The main screen shows all appointments for the current date.
2. **Confirm a Booking**: Click the "Confirm" button. This triggers an n8n webhook to notify the client.
3. **Mark as No-show**: If a client misses their appointment, click "No-show" to track loss metrics.

## Tracking ROI
- Check the **Metrics** panel to see your current revenue and no-show rate.
- Compare "Sin Sistema" vs "Con VERTEX" to see your monthly savings.

## Customizing your Business
- Toggle between **Salón** and **Taller** modes to change available services.
- Switch between **Light** and **Dark** modes for better visibility.
