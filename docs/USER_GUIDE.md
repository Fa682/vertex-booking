# USER GUIDE - VERTEX BOOKING

## Introduction
Welcome to VERTEX Booking! This guide will help you set up and use the platform to manage your business appointments effectively.

## Booking an Appointment
1. **Enter your Name and Phone**: We use your phone number to send WhatsApp reminders.
2. **Select a Service**: Choose from the available services listed.
3. **Choose a Date and Time**: Select when you'd like to visit the business.
4. **Confirm**: Click "Agendar Cita". You will receive a WhatsApp confirmation shortly.

## Managing your Bookings (Admin)
- Access the **Citas de Hoy** section to see your schedule.
- Click **Confirmar** when you see a new appointment.
- Use **No-show** to track clients who didn't arrive.

## Switching Themes
- Click the **☀️ Light** or **🌙 Dark** button at the top right to change the look of the app.
