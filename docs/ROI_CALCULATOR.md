# ROI CALCULATOR - VERTEX BOOKING

## Inputs
- **Current Monthly Bookings**: 100
- **Current No-show Rate**: 35%
- **Average Price per Service**: $150 MXN

## Calculation
1. **Lost Revenue (Current)**: 100 × 0.35 × 150 = **$5,250 MXN**
2. **Lost Revenue (With VERTEX)**: 100 × 0.08 × 150 = **$1,200 MXN**
3. **Monthly Savings**: 5,250 - 1,200 = **$4,050 MXN**

## Result
By using VERTEX, you save **$4,050 MXN per month**.
Investment: $3,960 USD.
ROI achieved in approximately 27 days of usage by a typical salon.
